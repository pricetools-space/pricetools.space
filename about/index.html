pricetools.space is a growing collection of tools I put together to help make daily life easier living on a bitcoin standard. If you find it useful, please consider sending a donation to pricetools@getalby.com.

Free to use, open source under MIT license. No personal data is collected or stored. All current and historical prices are served from an internal database with updated price information provided by mempool.space. For any questions or suggestions, send me a message.
